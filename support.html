How do I add a PDF?

Tap Import PDF in your library and choose a file from Files. You can also open a PDF in PDF Speak from another app when offered by iOS. The app keeps its own copy. Duplicate copies are detected.

Why can’t I listen to a document?

Narration needs readable text. Scanned images may require text recognition; results depend on scan quality, language, and layout. Complex columns, handwriting, equations, and tables may not read in the intended order. Password-protected files need an unlocked copy before import.

How do I change the voice?

Open Settings → Narration voice, or Voice in the full player. Additional voices can be downloaded through iOS accessibility speech settings. Install your preferred voice and test it without a network connection before relying on offline listening.

Why is Apple AI unavailable?

Apple AI needs iOS 26 or later, an eligible device, Apple Intelligence enabled, and the system model ready in a supported language. Settings → On-device intelligence shows availability and a real generation test. Reading, listening, and text search remain usable without Apple AI. AI can make mistakes; check the linked source pages.

What’s the difference between Ask AI and Search text?

Ask AI generates an answer using supplied PDF passages when available. Search text finds matching words and returns source excerpts. Questions currently work independently, so include the subject in follow-up questions.

Can I use AI without Apple Intelligence?

The optional Qwen3 0.6B model supports English documents and questions in this release and can be downloaded in Settings → On-device intelligence. It is about 343 MB, requires at least 6 GB of device memory and about 800 MB of free storage, and generates answers locally after download. You can pause or resume a download and remove the model. After installation, choose Local AI, then close and reopen the document assistant. A small model can make mistakes; check source pages. Physical-device speed and memory use vary.

How do I report an AI answer?

Tap Report answer below a generated response, choose a reason, and review the preview. Including the answer is optional and off by default. PDFs, titles, questions, source excerpts, and other messages are not automatically attached. Review any sensitive information before sending. If Mail is unavailable, copy the report and email admin@technoforbes.com yourself. We review concerns through support. Do not send passwords or private documents.

AI usage and safety

Use AI to understand documents you are entitled to use. AI may make mistakes; compare its answers with the original PDF. Answers are not professional advice or a basis for medical, legal, financial, employment, or other high-stakes decisions. Do not use AI to exploit children, generate sexual content, facilitate violence or self-harm, harass others, commit fraud, steal information, or bypass safety protections. Educational discussion and prevention information are welcome. Safety checks can miss harmful content or block legitimate requests; report problems rather than changing providers to bypass a refusal. The in-app AI usage & safety page explains these limits and privacy choices.

Cleaner PDF listening

Settings → Cleaner listening offers automatic two-column reading order and options to skip repeated headers/footers, page numbers, small-print footnotes, citations, or web addresses. Changes apply after reopening the PDF. These tools use layout heuristics and can make mistakes on complex documents; turn an option off if it omits something important. The original PDF and its searchable text are preserved.

Highlights, notes, and audio exports

Select PDF text and tap Highlight, or use a passage’s context menu in Read along to highlight and add a note. PDF actions → Highlights & notes lets you edit, delete, jump to a source, or export notes. PDF actions → Export narration generates M4A audio for your chosen page range with the installed voice and listening speed. Keep the app open during generation. A successful saved audio file uses one of the ten shared daily free export uses; cancellation does not consume one. Personal Voice is not supported for export.

Optional iCloud sync

Open Settings → iCloud sync, read the explanation, and confirm to enable it. Use the same Apple account on your devices, enable iCloud Drive for PDF Speak, and allow time for transfers. PDFs, positions, bookmarks, highlights, passage notes, and saved study material sync; chats, model downloads, temporary audio, and daily counters do not. Sync now retries pending work. If files are waiting, check connectivity, iCloud storage, and account settings. Turning sync off keeps local and existing iCloud copies. With sync on, deleting a PDF queues deletion on your synced devices. Keep independent copies of important originals.

Where are my files and saved places?

Your imported PDF, bookmarks, positions, conversations, highlights, and study material are stored locally; selected reading data also syncs if you enable iCloud. Delete a PDF from its library menu to remove its associated data. Clear the assistant conversation using its trash button. Uninstalling removes the local library but keeps existing iCloud copies; delete with sync enabled or manage iCloud app storage if you also want to remove those. Keep originals elsewhere.

The app stopped responding or a PDF won’t open

Close and reopen the app, confirm free device storage, and try another PDF to see whether the issue is file-specific. For a locked PDF, export an unlocked copy using a tool you trust. Email us with the steps and any error text if the problem continues.
