Effective 18 November 2026

Your PDFs belong to you.

This policy explains how PDF Speak handles information. For privacy questions, contact admin@technoforbes.com.

Information processed in the app

PDF Speak stores imported PDF copies, extracted or recognized text, document titles, bookmarks, reading and listening positions, app preferences, and assistant conversations locally in the app’s storage. We do not operate a server that receives these documents or conversations. The app does not require an account and includes no developer-operated advertising, analytics, or tracking.

Reading, text recognition, speech, and AI

PDF rendering and text extraction use Apple platform frameworks. Text recognition for scanned pages, where offered, runs on the device. Narration uses iOS voices; voice assets may need to be downloaded through system settings. Apple on-device AI is optional and depends on device eligibility, system settings, language, and model availability. Document passages supplied to this provider are processed using Apple’s on-device system language model. The app does not send them to a developer-operated remote AI service.

Optional local model download

If you choose the optional Qwen3 local AI model, the app connects to Hugging Face and its file-delivery providers to download model weights. These services receive ordinary connection information such as your IP address. The app does not attach your PDF, questions, or conversations to that download. Downloaded model files remain local, are excluded from cloud backup, and can be removed in Settings. Local model answers are generated on the device. See Hugging Face’s Privacy Policy.

Files you choose to import or share

You control which files you import. Selecting an item from iCloud Drive or another file provider may cause that provider to download it under its own policies. When you choose Share PDF, iOS sends the selected PDF to the destination you choose; that destination handles it under its own policy. PDF Speak does not automatically publish your documents.

Optional iCloud sync

Sync is off by default. If you enable it in Settings and confirm, PDF Speak copies your PDFs, library titles, reading/listening positions, bookmarks, highlights, passage notes, and saved study material to the app’s private iCloud Drive container for your Apple account. Other PDF Speak devices signed in to that account can download those copies. Apple processes and stores this information under its iCloud terms and privacy policy; it is not sent to the developer or to an AI generation service. Sync depends on account availability, internet access, and iCloud storage. Assistant chats, downloaded AI weights, temporary narration audio, and daily free-use counters are not synced.

Turning sync off stops further exchanges while keeping local files and existing iCloud copies. An Apple account change stops sync until you review and enable it again. While sync is on, deleting a PDF queues deletion of its PDF, reading metadata, annotations, and study material from iCloud and synced devices. Minimal deletion records containing file identifiers and deletion times are retained to stop offline devices restoring deleted data. Earlier note versions may be kept to avoid losing conflicting edits. Copies you exported outside the app are separate and must be deleted at their destinations. Uninstalling removes local files but does not remove existing iCloud copies; manage those through iCloud storage settings or delete PDFs with sync enabled before uninstalling.

Study and audio exports

Flashcards, quizzes, AI notes, and tutor replies are generated on the device. Personal notes and review progress are stored with the document. If you export study material, its questions, answers, notes, and source excerpts are included in the selected PDF, Markdown, or CSV file. Narration export creates an M4A file locally using an installed system voice. You choose the destination in the system save picker. Temporary audio is removed after completion or cancellation. A destination such as iCloud Drive, another file provider, or an app may sync or share your exported files under its own settings and policies.

Storage, retention, and deletion

Local documents, extraction caches, bookmarks, progress, conversations, highlights, notes, and study material are kept in app-private storage and excluded from automatic cloud backup. iCloud sync is a separate optional feature described above. Delete a document from the library to remove its associated local data. Clear a conversation in the assistant to remove that history. Uninstalling the app removes its local library. App settings are stored using iOS preferences. Keep separate copies of important originals. Optional iCloud sync is not a full-library backup or archive service.

Permissions and security

The app uses the system file picker to access selected files and does not need your microphone to read text aloud. Local document data uses iOS file protection. Device security, backups outside the app, and any copies you export are also affected by your operating system and chosen services. No storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

AI answer reports

Reports are optional and are not sent automatically. The in-app report preview includes your selected reason, provider name, and any description you enter. Including the answer is off by default. If you turn it on, the answer may contain information from your PDF. The app does not automatically attach the PDF file, document title, questions, source excerpts, or other conversation messages. You review and send the email using Mail or your preferred email app, or explicitly copy the report. Email and clipboard services operate under their own settings and policies. Reports received by support are handled as support correspondence below.

Support communications

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and attachments through our email provider. We use them to respond and resolve your request, and retain them only as needed for support and applicable obligations. Please do not send sensitive PDFs, passwords, or payment information. You may ask us to delete support correspondence, subject to legal retention requirements.

Apple and website services

Apple may process App Store, device diagnostic, and system-service information under its own policies and your settings. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which receives standard connection information such as IP addresses when serving pages. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement and Apple’s Privacy Policy. This site contains no analytics script, advertising tracker, contact form, or nonessential cookie.

Children and changes

The app does not ask for age or knowingly collect children’s personal information through an account or analytics service. A parent or guardian may contact us about support correspondence. We may update this policy when the app or its practices change; the effective date above identifies this version.

Your choices and contact

You can use core reading and listening without AI, delete local documents, choose whether to share files, and contact admin@technoforbes.com about privacy or support correspondence.
